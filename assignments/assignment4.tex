\textbf{Relevant reading}: ILA 4.1--4.4, 6.1. Problems marked \textbf{[code]} should be submitted as a program in a language of your choice (Julia or Python recommended). The cited problem numbers are from ILA, 5th edition (Strang, 2016).

\begin{enumerate}
\item ILA, Problem Set 4.2: problems 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 13, 17, 21, 22, 23, 28, 31, 32, \emph{and} one extra credit problem: 34.
\item Let $\bm{a} \in \Real^n$ be nonzero and let $\bm{b} \in \Real^n$. Prove that the
  projection $\bm{p} = \frac{\bm{a}\tr \bm{b}}{\bm{a}\tr \bm{a}} \, \bm{a}$ is the unique
  point on the line through $\bm{a}$ closest to $\bm{b}$; that is,
  $\|\bm{b} - \bm{p}\| < \|\bm{b} - x\bm{a}\|$ for every scalar $x$ with $x\bm{a} \neq \bm{p}$.
\item Let $\bm{A}$ be an $m \times n$ matrix with orthonormal columns. Show that the matrix
  projecting onto the column space of $\bm{A}$ simplifies from
  $\bm{P} = \bm{A}(\bm{A}\tr\bm{A})^{-1}\bm{A}\tr$ to $\bm{P} = \bm{A}\bm{A}\tr$, so that no
  matrix inverse needs to be computed.
\item ILA, Problem Set 6.1: problems 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 19, 22, 30, 37.
\item \textbf{[code]} Let $\bm{a} = (2,1)\tr$ and let $\bm{P}$ be the matrix that projects
  onto the line through $\bm{a}$. Build $\bm{P}$ in code and check numerically that
  $\bm{P}\tr = \bm{P}$ and $\bm{P}^2 = \bm{P}$. Compute the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of
  $\bm{P}$, and explain why the eigenvalues must be $1$ and $0$ and what their eigenvectors
  are geometrically. Finally, plot the line through $\bm{a}$ together with two or three
  vectors $\bm{b}$ of your choice and their projections $\bm{P}\bm{b}$, joining each
  $\bm{b}$ to $\bm{P}\bm{b}$ by a dashed segment, and confirm from the plot that
  $\bm{b} - \bm{P}\bm{b}$ is perpendicular to the line.
\item \textbf{Time spent.} Report approximately how many hours you spent completing this problem set (including the reading).
\end{enumerate}
